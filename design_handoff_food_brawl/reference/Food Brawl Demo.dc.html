Every restaurant name here is invented — stand-ins for the Google Places response, shaped exactly as the real query returns them. None of it ships: spotsFor() is the single seam the live query replaces. The almanac deliberately stores only the food and the date, never a restaurant name, which Places terms don't permit keeping. The corgis, the track and the ingredient avatars are all your artwork; photo strips are empty frames the real build fills from the Places photo references. Deep links show the URL they would fire rather than leaving the page.
